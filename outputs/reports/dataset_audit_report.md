## 5. Critical Leakage Findings in Zenodo Dataset
> [!WARNING]
> Our provenance audit revealed that the Zenodo 5,400 image collection derived its images directly from Mendeley and Kaggle, applied offline rotations and flips to synthesize 1,260 images per class, and distributed augmented variants of identical patients across train, val, and test splits (e.g. `N26` and `OS1` variants appear simultaneously in train and test).
> **Research Remedy**: We use Mendeley's 239 authentic unaugmented examinations with documented patient IDs as our primary gold-standard cohort for patient-isolated evaluation, preventing test-set contamination.
